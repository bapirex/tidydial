First release of TidyDial.

* Format contact phone numbers as International, National, E.164 or RFC 3966
* Automatic country detection with a searchable country picker
* Review list grouped by contact, with before and after for each number, filters and search
* Update only the numbers you select, and undo the last update
* Skips messaging-app accounts, emergency numbers and short codes by default
* Material 3 Expressive design with dynamic color and dark theme
* Works fully offline, with no internet permission
