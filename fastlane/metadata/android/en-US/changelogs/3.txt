* Call any number from TidyDial: open your phone app, or call through WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram, Google Meet and other apps that offer it
* Numbers already grouped with spaces are no longer reshuffled
* Toll-free and other domestic-only numbers no longer get a country code, which stopped them connecting
* National format keeps the country code where calls need it (Brazil, Mexico, Chile, Uzbekistan) and keeps Brazilian carrier codes
