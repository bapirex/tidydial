<p>TidyDial gives every phone number in your contacts one clean, consistent format. You review every change before anything is written, and you can undo the last update.</p>

<p><b>How it works</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Pick a format: International (+1 212-555-0199), National ((212) 555-0199), E.164 (+12125550199) or RFC 3966 (tel:+1-212-555-0199).</li>
<li>Numbers without a country code are read as local to your country. TidyDial detects the country from your SIM, network or language settings, and you can change it at any time.</li>
<li>Your numbers are grouped by contact, and each one shows its current and new form. Filter by changes, selection or invalid numbers, or search by name or number.</li>
<li>Choose exactly which numbers to update, then confirm. Only the phone number field is changed. Names, photos and everything else stay as they are.</li>
<li>Undo the last update, even after restarting the app.</li>
<li>Optional backups: before each update, TidyDial can save the old and new numbers in a folder you choose, such as Documents. They stay there after uninstalling, and you can restore from them. If you pick a cloud storage app's folder, that app syncs the files.</li>
<li>Tap the call button next to a number to open your phone app with the number filled in, or to call through another app that offers it.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Careful by default</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Only numbers that can be recognized are rewritten. Anything else is flagged and left alone.</li>
<li>Emergency numbers (112, 911), short service numbers and codes such as *#06# are never changed.</li>
<li>Extensions after a pause or wait are kept.</li>
<li>Numbers synced by messaging apps (WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal and others) are skipped by default, because those apps overwrite edits with their own copy. You can choose which accounts to include.</li>
<li>A number that was changed somewhere else after TidyDial read it is skipped, not overwritten.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Private and offline</b></p>
<ul>
<li>No internet permission. TidyDial cannot send your contacts anywhere.</li>
<li>No ads, trackers, analytics or proprietary libraries.</li>
<li>Phone numbers are parsed on your device with Google's open-source libphonenumber library.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Permissions</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Read contacts: to list your phone numbers and show how they would be formatted.</li>
<li>Write contacts: to save the numbers you choose to update, and to restore them when you undo or restore a backup.</li>
<li>Make phone calls: only requested when you choose to call through another app such as WhatsApp. Regular calls open your phone app.</li>
</ul>
<p>These are the only permissions TidyDial asks you for.</p>

<p>Built with Jetpack Compose and Material 3 Expressive. It uses dynamic color (Material You) on Android 12 and later, and has a dark theme. Requires Android 8.0 or later.</p>
